Saturday Brunch for Four

Everything on the table at ten. The bacon cooks in the oven so the stove is free for pancakes; the fruit salad and the coffee fill the gaps. One person can do this in about 45 minutes.

Bacon
Lay a pound of bacon on a rack over a sheet pan and bake at 400°F for 20 minutes, until crisp, turning the pan once halfway. Drain on paper towels for 2 minutes; it stays warm for a while.

Pancakes
Whisk together two cups of flour, two tablespoons of sugar, a tablespoon of baking powder and a teaspoon of salt; 5 minutes. In a jug beat two eggs with a cup and a half of milk and three tablespoons of melted butter, then stir the wet into the dry until just combined, another 5 minutes; lumps are fine. Let the batter rest for 10 minutes while the bacon cooks. Heat a griddle over medium heat for 5 minutes. Cook the pancakes in batches, a quarter cup each, about 2 minutes per side, 15 minutes for the whole batch.

Fruit salad
Halve a pint of strawberries, cube a small melon and a mango, and add a cup of blueberries; this takes about 10 minutes. Squeeze half a lime over the top and chill for at least 15 minutes.

Coffee
Grind the beans and set up the pot, 3 minutes, then brew a full pot, which takes 8 minutes, timed to finish as the pancakes come off the griddle.

Serve
Bring everything out together while the pancakes are hot; 2 minutes.
