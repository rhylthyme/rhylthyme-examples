Load-In Schedule — The Lyric, one-nighter

One loading dock, one stage, one lighting desk, one PA, one monitor world. Crew call is 8am and that is where this schedule starts. The dock takes one truck at a time, so the lighting truck empties before the backline truck pulls in, and nothing goes up on stage while the dock is still moving through it.

Dock
Lighting truck in: bars, fixtures, cable, 60 minutes.
Backline truck in the moment the lighting truck is empty: 45 minutes.
Clear the dock and get the empties out; 15 minutes.

Lighting
Rig the bars as soon as the lighting truck is off the dock: 45 minutes on stage.
Focus from the desk once the rig is up: 60 minutes.
Build the cues after focus: 30 minutes if the plot is clean, up to 90 if it is not.
Strike the rig when the show is down: 60 minutes.

PA
Fly the PA as soon as the lighting truck is off the dock: 75 minutes.
Ring out the house: 20 minutes.

Monitors
Set monitor world once the backline truck is off the dock: 45 minutes.
Ring out the wedges: 20 minutes.

Band
Backline up as soon as the dock is clear of the second truck: 45 minutes on stage.
Soundcheck when the house and the wedges are both rung out: 60 minutes, drums first.
Band dinner after soundcheck: 60 minutes in the green room.

House
Set front of house from two hours in: 60 minutes, merch, bars, ushers.
Clean the house after the band go to dinner; 30 minutes.
Doors when the house manager calls them; 30 minutes of walk-up.
Show on stage once the house is in, and it runs as long as it runs.
Backline out fifteen minutes after the show is down: 45 minutes on the dock.

Notes
The dock is the first bottleneck and the stage is the second. Focus and cue-build both want the desk, so they are strictly sequential. Soundcheck is the only step in the day that waits on two separate departments at once.
