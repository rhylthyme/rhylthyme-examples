Weekday Lentil Soup and Salad for Four

Lunch at half past twelve. One cook, two burners, one toaster oven, one stick blender. Nothing here is fussy; the only real constraint is that the cook can stand in one place at a time, so the salad and the bread wait until the soup is quietly simmering.

Soup
Dice an onion, a carrot and two sticks of celery; 8 minutes. Sweat them in oil with a bay leaf for 10 minutes. Stir in the rinsed lentils, a spoonful of tomato paste and a litre of stock; 3 minutes. Simmer until the lentils are soft, 25 to 40 minutes. Blitz a third of the pot with the stick blender and stir it back in; 4 minutes. Season it, then keep it at a bare simmer until you sit down.

Salad
Start the salad once the soup is simmering. Wash and spin the leaves; 5 minutes. Shave the fennel and the radishes thin; 7 minutes. Whisk the lemon, oil and mustard in the bottom of the bowl; 3 minutes. Five minutes after the soup goes back on the heat, dress and toss the salad; 2 minutes. Dressed any earlier and the leaves collapse.

Bread
Cut the loaf into thick slices once the soup is simmering; 3 minutes. Warm them in the toaster oven for 8 minutes. Wrap the warm bread in a cloth and leave it on the table.

Table
Lay four places once the bread is wrapped; 5 minutes. When the soup, the salad and the bread are all ready, carry the pot and the bowl through together; 3 minutes.
