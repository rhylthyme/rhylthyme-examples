Practice Plan — Thursday AM, three lanes

We have three lanes for two and a half hours and one set of starting blocks. Lane 1 is the sprint group, lane 2 the middle distance, lane 3 the distance group. Everything except the sprint set runs in all three lanes at once; the sprint set runs one lane at a time off the blocks, so lanes wait their turn.

On deck
Brief the squad on the pool deck: what the morning is, who is in which lane, 5 minutes.
Watch the warm-up from the deck and fix anything ugly; 15 minutes.

Warm-up
All three lanes start together: 20 minutes, 800 easy, mixed stroke, breathing every five on the last hundred.

Whiteboard
Call the lanes to the wall when the warm-up is done and walk the main set on the whiteboard; 5 minutes. Nobody pushes off until the board is done.

Kick set
20 minutes of kick, boards for lanes 1 and 2, vertical kick for lane 3, once the whiteboard talk is over.

Main set
Lane 1: 30 to 50 minutes, 40 minutes is the plan.
Lane 2: 30 to 50 minutes, 45 minutes is the plan.
Lane 3: 30 to 50 minutes, 50 minutes is the plan.
Every lane swims its own main set and the sprint set does not start until the last lane is in.

Sprint set
One set of blocks, one lane at a time, 10 minutes each.
Lane 1 goes first, off the blocks, dive starts, 25s flat out.
Lane 2 goes second, same thing, on the lane 1 group's last dive.
Lane 3 goes third and gets the extra rest it will need.
Time the sprints from the deck: 30 minutes, stopwatch and the wall clock.

Cooldown
All three lanes cool down together once the last sprint is in: 10 minutes, 300 easy backstroke and breaststroke.

Debrief
10 minutes on the deck at the end: splits, what to fix, Saturday's meet.

Note
The main set is the only place a lane can run long and the only place the plan gives them room to. Everything after it is a join: the blocks cannot be shared and the cooldown starts together or it does not start at all.
