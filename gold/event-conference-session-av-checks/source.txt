Session Chair's Sheet — Room 2B, Thursday morning

One room, one projector, one lapel pack, one AV desk, one chair. Three talks, thirty minutes each, with questions after every one. The chair calls each speaker to the lectern; nothing starts until they do.

Before the room opens
Line check at the AV desk: laptop, HDMI, house sound, record feed. 30 minutes.
Test the projector once the desk is up: resolution, keystone, the cue slide. 15 minutes.
Check the lapel pack after the projector: battery, gain, a walk to the back row. 10 minutes.

Slides
Load the first deck once the lapel pack is checked; 10 minutes, and leave it on the cue slide.
Load the second deck as soon as the first talk's questions are over; 5 minutes.
Load the third deck while the coffee is going out; 5 minutes.

The session
Chair opens the room, housekeeping and fire exits; 5 minutes.
First talk when the chair calls the first speaker: 30 minutes.
Questions after the first talk: five to ten minutes, seven is the plan.
Changeover to the second speaker: unclip the pack, clip it on, 5 minutes.
Second talk when the chair calls the second speaker: 30 minutes.
Questions after the second talk: five to ten minutes, seven is the plan.
Changeover to the third speaker once the coffee is cleared from the foyer; 5 minutes.
Third talk when the chair calls the third speaker: 30 minutes.
Questions after the third talk: five to fifteen minutes, ten is the plan.
Chair closes the session; 5 minutes.

Catering
Set the coffee station in the service corridor from ninety minutes in; 30 minutes.
Coffee goes out to the foyer when the second talk's questions end; 20 minutes.
Clear the foyer; 10 minutes.

Strike
Strike the AV desk when the chair closes; 20 minutes, cables coiled, record feed off.

Notes
The projector is the contended thing, not the room: a deck cannot be loaded while a talk is on it, so every load sits in a gap. The lapel pack is the other one, and it is the reason changeovers are real steps rather than slack. Question time is the only elastic in the morning; if the first two run long the chair trims the third.
