Sheet-Pan Chicken with Potatoes and Broccoli

A Tuesday dinner for four. One oven, two sheet pans, one burner, one cook. The potatoes need longer than the chicken and the broccoli needs least of all, so the two pans go in at different times and come out together.

Oven
Heat the oven to 425°F; 15 minutes. While it heats, toss the halved potatoes with oil and salt on the first sheet pan; 8 minutes. Slide the potatoes in and roast them for 15 minutes on their own.

Pat the chicken thighs dry, rub them with the spice mix and lay them on the second pan; 10 minutes. Put the second pan in when the potatoes have had their 15 minutes. Twenty minutes after the chicken goes in, pull both pans out, rotate them, tip the broccoli onto the potato pan and put everything back; 4 minutes. Roast for a final 15 minutes, until the thighs read 175°F and the broccoli is charred at the edges. Rest the chicken on a board for 8 minutes before slicing.

Pan sauce
Once the pans are out, tip the pan juices into a small pan, add stock and mustard and reduce for 6 minutes. Whisk in cold butter off the heat; 2 minutes. Keep the sauce off the flame until you plate.

Salad
Start the salad when the chicken goes in the oven, not before, or the leaves will sit around. Wash and dry the leaves; 6 minutes. Shake the vinaigrette together in a jar; 3 minutes. Dress the salad only when the chicken has been resting five minutes; 2 minutes.

Serve
Slice the thighs and plate everything; 6 minutes. Sauce goes over the chicken at the table.
