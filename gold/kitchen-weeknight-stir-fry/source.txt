Weeknight Chicken and Broccoli Stir-Fry with Rice

Serves two. You have two burners and about 35 minutes, and one person cooking. Rice goes on first because it takes the longest; the stir-fry is quick and should go from wok to plate in under a minute.

Rice
Rinse one cup of jasmine rice until the water runs clear, about 2 minutes. Put it in a small pot with one and a quarter cups of water and bring to a boil over high heat, about 5 minutes. Cover, turn the heat to low and simmer for 15 minutes. Take it off the heat and let it steam, lid on, for 10 minutes. Fluff with a fork just before serving; 1 minute.

Prep
While the rice comes to a boil, slice a pound of chicken thighs into thin strips, about 5 minutes. Toss the strips with a tablespoon of soy sauce and a teaspoon of cornstarch and let them sit for 10 minutes. While the chicken marinates, cut a head of broccoli into small florets and mince three cloves of garlic and a thumb of ginger; 5 minutes. Stir together the sauce in a small bowl: two tablespoons of soy sauce, one of oyster sauce, a teaspoon of sugar and a splash of rice vinegar; 2 minutes.

Stir-fry
When the rice comes off the heat to steam, heat a wok over the highest flame until it smokes, about 3 minutes. Add oil and the chicken and stir-fry for 4 minutes until browned; remove to a plate. Add the broccoli with a splash of water, cover, and steam for 3 minutes. Uncover, add the garlic and ginger and cook for 30 seconds, then return the chicken, pour in the sauce and toss for 1 minute until glossy.

Serve immediately over the rice.
