Plasmid Miniprep, Restriction Digest and Gel Check

This protocol takes a 5 mL overnight culture of E. coli carrying a pUC-based plasmid through a spin-column miniprep, a diagnostic double digest, and an agarose gel. The bench has one benchtop centrifuge, one microcentrifuge, one 37 °C heat block, one 65 °C heat block, a microwave, one spectrophotometer and a single gel rig, so plan the shared equipment: the gel can be cast while the culture is spinning down, and the ladder can be prepared during the digest. One person runs the whole protocol.

Part 1: Miniprep
1. Pellet the culture: transfer 5 mL of the overnight culture to a 15 mL tube and centrifuge at 4,000 × g for 10 minutes. Discard the supernatant.
2. Resuspend the pellet in 250 µL of Resuspension Buffer (with RNase A) by vortexing; about 2 minutes.
3. Add 250 µL of Lysis Buffer, invert gently 6 times and incubate at room temperature for 5 minutes. Do not exceed 5 minutes.
4. Add 350 µL of Neutralization Buffer, invert 6 times, and centrifuge at maximum speed for 10 minutes in the microcentrifuge.
5. Load the cleared lysate onto a spin column and spin for 1 minute. Discard the flow-through.
6. Wash the column with 700 µL of Wash Buffer, spin for 1 minute, discard the flow-through, then spin the empty column for 2 minutes to dry the membrane.
7. Transfer the column to a clean tube, add 50 µL of Elution Buffer, wait 1 minute, and spin for 1 minute to elute.
8. Measure the concentration on the spectrophotometer; 3 minutes.

Part 2: Agarose gel
9. While the culture is pelleting, weigh 0.5 g of agarose into 50 mL of 1× TAE and microwave for 2 minutes, swirling once, until fully dissolved.
10. Cool the flask on the bench for 10 minutes, then add 5 µL of DNA stain and pour the gel into the casting tray with the comb in place; 2 minutes.
11. Let the gel set for 30 minutes at room temperature, then remove the comb and place the gel in the rig under 1× TAE; 2 minutes.

Part 3: Digest
12. In a 0.2 mL tube combine 500 ng of plasmid, 2 µL of 10× buffer, 0.5 µL each of EcoRI and HindIII, and water to 20 µL; 5 minutes of pipetting.
13. Incubate at 37 °C for 60 minutes in the heat block.
14. Heat-inactivate the enzymes at 65 °C for 20 minutes.
15. Ten minutes into the digest, thaw the 1 kb ladder and mix 4 µL of 6× loading dye into a fresh tube for each lane; 5 minutes.
16. Add loading dye to the digest, briefly spin down the tube in the microcentrifuge for 30 seconds, and load 10 µL per well alongside 5 µL of ladder; 5 minutes.
17. Run the gel at 100 V for 45 minutes, until the dye front is two-thirds down the gel.
18. Image the gel on the transilluminator and record band sizes; 5 minutes.
