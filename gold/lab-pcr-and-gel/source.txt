Colony PCR Screen with a Diagnostic Gel

Eight candidate clones are screened by PCR and the products are run on a single agarose gel. The bench has one thermocycler, one microcentrifuge, a microwave, one gel rig and one transilluminator, and one person runs the whole thing. The cycling block is long enough to cast and set the gel while it runs, so start the agarose partway through the run rather than waiting for the machine to finish.

Part 1: Reaction setup
1. Thaw the primers, dNTPs and 10× buffer on ice; 15 minutes. Keep the polymerase in the cold block until the last moment.
2. Assemble a 25 µL master mix on ice for eight reactions plus one spare; 10 minutes of pipetting.
3. Aliquot 25 µL into eight strip tubes, add 1 µL of each colony suspension and spin them down for 30 seconds in the microcentrifuge; 5 minutes.

Part 2: Cycling
4. Load the strip tubes into the thermocycler and start the program; 3 minutes.
5. Run 95 °C for 3 minutes, then 35 cycles of 95 °C for 30 seconds, 58 °C for 30 seconds and 72 °C for 45 seconds, then a final 72 °C for 5 minutes. The block reports 1 hour 50 minutes.

Part 3: Agarose gel
6. Twenty-five minutes into the cycling run, weigh 1.0 g of agarose into 100 mL of 1× TAE and microwave for 3 minutes, swirling once, until no lens of undissolved agarose is left.
7. Cool the flask on the bench for 12 minutes, until it is comfortable to hold.
8. Add 5 µL of DNA stain, pour the gel into the casting tray and seat the 10-well comb; 3 minutes.
9. Let the gel set for 35 minutes at room temperature. Do not rush this; a soft gel tears at the wells.
10. Remove the comb and place the gel in the rig under 1× TAE; 2 minutes.

Part 4: Run and read
11. Ten minutes after the gel is poured to set, thaw the 100 bp ladder and mix it with 6× loading dye; 5 minutes.
12. When the cycling finishes, spin the tubes down and add 5 µL of loading dye to each; 5 minutes.
13. Load 10 µL per well alongside 5 µL of ladder; 8 minutes. Nothing goes into a well until the gel is in the rig and the ladder is ready.
14. Run the gel at 120 V for 40 minutes, until the dye front is two-thirds down.
15. Image the gel on the transilluminator; 5 minutes.
16. Record which clones carry the insert and mark the plate; 10 minutes.
