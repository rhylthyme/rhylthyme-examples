Sandwich ELISA on Two Plates, One Washer

Two 96-well plates are run half an hour apart through a sandwich ELISA for a secreted cytokine. There is one automated plate washer, one 37 °C plate incubator that holds two plates, one plate reader and one operator. The washer is the bottleneck: no two plates can be in it at once, and every incubation is bracketed by a wash.

Day 1
1. Coat plate 1 with 100 µL per well of capture antibody in carbonate buffer, seal it and leave it in the cold room at 4 °C overnight. Take it out when you are ready to start, not before.
2. Coat plate 2 the same way half an hour later, so the two plates stay half an hour apart all day.

Day 2, reagents
3. Fifteen hours into the coat, make up the seven-point standard curve in assay diluent; 30 minutes.
4. Dilute the samples 1:4 into assay diluent and lay out the plate map; 30 minutes.

Day 2, plate 1
5. Wash the plate four times with 300 µL of wash buffer; 5 minutes on the washer.
6. Block with 200 µL per well of assay diluent for 1 hour at 37 °C.
7. Wash four times; 5 minutes on the washer.
8. Add 100 µL of standards and samples and incubate for 2 hours at 37 °C. Nothing goes on the plate until the dilutions are laid out.
9. Wash four times; 5 minutes on the washer.
10. Add 100 µL of biotinylated detection antibody and incubate for 1 hour at 37 °C.
11. Wash four times; 5 minutes on the washer.
12. Add 100 µL of TMB substrate, develop in the dark for exactly 15 minutes and stop with 50 µL of 2 N sulfuric acid.
13. Read the absorbance at 450 nm with a 570 nm reference; 5 minutes on the reader.

Day 2, plate 2
14. Wash plate 2 four times as soon as the washer is free; 5 minutes.
15. Block plate 2 with 200 µL of assay diluent for 1 hour at 37 °C.
16. Wash plate 2 again once plate 1 is off the washer; 5 minutes.
17. Add the standards and samples to plate 2 and incubate for 2 hours at 37 °C.
18. Wash plate 2; 5 minutes.
19. Add the detection antibody to plate 2 and incubate for 1 hour at 37 °C.
20. Wash plate 2 a final time; 5 minutes.
21. Develop plate 2 with TMB for exactly 15 minutes and stop it.
22. Read plate 2 at 450 nm; 5 minutes.
