Partner Upper-Body Superset Session

Two lifters, one flat bench with a barbell, one pair of dumbbells, one pull-up bar and two rowing ergs. The whole session should take about 25 minutes. You work as a pair: while Partner A is on the first exercise of a superset, Partner B is on the second, and then you swap; nobody waits for equipment if you keep the order.

Warm-up
Both partners start together with 5 minutes of easy rowing on the ergs, followed by 3 minutes of arm circles and band pull-aparts.

Superset A: bench press and dumbbell row (2 rounds)
Partner A loads the bar and does a set of 8 bench presses, which takes about 40 seconds. Partner B starts on the dumbbell row at the same time, 8 per side, also about 40 seconds. Then swap: A goes to the rows once B has finished with the dumbbells, and B takes the bench as soon as A racks the bar. After both exercises, rest 90 seconds before the next round. Do 2 rounds, resting after each.

Superset B: overhead press and pull-ups (2 rounds)
Move straight on after the last rest. Partner A does 8 strict overhead presses with the dumbbells, about 45 seconds, while Partner B does a set of pull-ups, 6 to 10 reps, about 30 seconds. Swap when both are done: A moves to the pull-up bar, and B takes the dumbbells when A sets them down. Rest 2 minutes between rounds; there is no rest after the second round.

Cool-down
Finish together with 5 minutes of stretching: doorway chest stretch, lat stretch on the pull-up bar, and slow shoulder rolls.
