Breakfast for Six on One Griddle

One electric griddle, a two-slice toaster, a burr grinder, a drip coffee maker and an oven turned to its lowest setting to hold things warm. One cook, six people, everyone eating at nine.

Prep
Whisk the dry pancake ingredients together, then beat in the buttermilk and eggs; 10 minutes. Let the batter stand for 20 minutes while the griddle does its first job. Crack a dozen eggs into a jug and season them; 5 minutes. Set the table and lay out six places; 10 minutes. Put six plates in the oven at its lowest setting and leave them there until you call everyone.

Griddle
Everything cooks on the one griddle, in order. Heat the griddle to 375°F; 8 minutes. Lay the bacon out and cook it for 14 minutes, turning once, then move it to the warm oven. Scrape the griddle and wipe off the fat; 3 minutes. Pancakes go on as soon as the griddle is clean and the batter has rested, in three passes: the first pass takes 6 minutes, the second another 6, and the third 6 more. Slide each pass into the oven as it comes off. Drop the griddle to 300°F and give it 4 minutes to settle, then scramble the eggs slowly for 7 minutes.

Toast
The toaster takes two slices at a time. Start the first batch of toast when the last pancake pass goes on; 3 minutes. Run a second batch straight after; 3 minutes. Butter all of it; 4 minutes.

Coffee
Start the grinder ten minutes after the bacon goes on so the pot is fresh. Grind the beans; 2 minutes. Brew a full pot; 9 minutes.

Serve
Call everyone when the eggs, the toast and the coffee are all ready, and carry the warm plates through; 5 minutes.
