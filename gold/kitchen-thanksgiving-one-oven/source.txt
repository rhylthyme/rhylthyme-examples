Thanksgiving for Eight with One Oven

We eat at six o'clock sharp. There is one oven and four burners, and two of you in the kitchen, so the oven schedule decides everything else. The turkey owns the oven for most of the afternoon; the sides go in while it rests, one after another.

The turkey (14 pounds)
Take the turkey out of the refrigerator 40 minutes before you season it so it is not fridge-cold. Heat the oven to 325°F; give it 20 minutes to come up. Pat the bird dry, rub it all over with softened butter, salt and pepper, and tuck halved onions and thyme into the cavity; this takes about twenty minutes. Roast the turkey for 3 hours, until a thermometer in the thigh reads 165°F. Move the turkey to a carving board and let it rest, loosely tented with foil, for 40 minutes before carving. Carving takes about 15 minutes.

Gravy
As soon as the turkey comes out, pour the pan drippings into a saucepan, whisk in flour and cook for 3 minutes, then whisk in stock and simmer the gravy for 10 minutes until it coats a spoon. Keep it warm over the lowest flame until dinner.

Stuffing
Cut a loaf of day-old bread into cubes and spread them on a sheet pan; you can do this first thing. Twenty minutes before the turkey is due out, melt butter in a skillet and cook diced onion and celery for 10 minutes until soft. Toss the bread with the vegetables, sage and two cups of stock and turn it into a buttered dish; 10 minutes. Bake the stuffing at 375°F for 35 minutes the moment the turkey is out; the oven will already be hot.

Green bean casserole
About ten minutes after the turkey comes out, trim two pounds of green beans and blanch them for 4 minutes in salted boiling water, then drain. Make the mushroom sauce: cook sliced mushrooms in butter for 8 minutes, add cream and simmer for 5 minutes. Combine the beans and sauce in a baking dish and scatter fried onions on top; 5 minutes. Bake the casserole for 20 minutes as soon as the stuffing comes out.

Mashed potatoes
When the turkey comes out, peel and quarter four pounds of potatoes, about 15 minutes, cover with cold salted water and boil for 20 minutes until a knife slides in easily. Drain, then mash with warm cream and a stick of butter; give it 10 minutes. Hold the potatoes covered on the back burner until everything else is ready.

Dinner rolls
The rolls only need warming: slide them into the oven for 8 minutes right after the casserole comes out.

Serve
Carry everything to the table together.
