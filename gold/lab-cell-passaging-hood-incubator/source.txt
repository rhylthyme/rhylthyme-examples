Passaging Two Flasks of Adherent HEK293 Cells

Two T75 flasks at about 90 % confluence are split 1:6 into four new flasks. The room has one biosafety cabinet, one 37 °C water bath, one CO₂ incubator, one benchtop centrifuge and one microscope with a haemocytometer. Only one flask can be open in the cabinet at a time, so the two flasks queue for it while the centrifuge and the microscope stay free. Work with pre-warmed medium, PBS and trypsin-EDTA throughout; nothing cold touches the cells. One operator.

Preparation
1. Wipe down the cabinet, lay out pipettes and tubes, and let the blower run; 10 minutes.
2. Check both flasks on the microscope for confluence and contamination; 10 minutes.

Flask A
3. Aspirate the spent medium and rinse the monolayer with 5 mL of PBS; 4 minutes in the cabinet.
4. Add 2 mL of trypsin-EDTA, rock the flask and return it to the incubator for 5 minutes.
5. Knock the flask, check detachment and neutralise with 8 mL of medium; 5 minutes in the cabinet.
6. Transfer the suspension to a 15 mL tube and spin at 300 × g for 5 minutes.
7. Aspirate the supernatant and resuspend the pellet in 10 mL of fresh medium; 4 minutes.
8. Count the cells on the haemocytometer; 6 minutes.
9. Seed two new T75 flasks at 1:6 and label them; 6 minutes in the cabinet.
10. Return the new flasks to the incubator and leave them to settle overnight, at least 16 hours.

Flask B
11. Start flask B the moment flask A leaves the cabinet: aspirate and rinse with 5 mL of PBS; 4 minutes.
12. Trypsinise flask B in the incubator for 5 minutes.
13. Neutralise flask B as soon as flask A is out of the cabinet again; 5 minutes.
14. Spin flask B at 300 × g for 5 minutes once the centrifuge is free.
15. Resuspend the flask B pellet in 10 mL of fresh medium; 4 minutes.
16. Count flask B on the haemocytometer once flask A has been counted; 6 minutes.
17. Seed two more T75 flasks at 1:6 and label them; 6 minutes.
18. Return them to the incubator to settle overnight alongside the first pair.

Finishing
19. Wipe the cabinet down, discard the waste and switch the blower to standby; 10 minutes.
20. Write the passage number, the split ratio and the counts into the culture log; 5 minutes.
