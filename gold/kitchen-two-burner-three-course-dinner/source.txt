Three-Course Dinner for Six: Two Burners, One Oven

Guests sit down at half past seven. There are two burners, one oven and one cook, so the oven does the braise first and the tart second, and the burners are rarely both free.

Mise en place
Chop the mirepoix, trim the short ribs and measure the stock: forty minutes of knife work before anything goes on the heat.

Main: braised short ribs
Heat the oven to 300°F; give it 20 minutes. Sear the short ribs in a heavy pot over high heat for 12 minutes, turning once. Lift the ribs out, soften the mirepoix in the same pot for 8 minutes, add the wine and reduce it for 5 minutes, then return the ribs with the stock. Braise the ribs, covered, for 3 hours. Rest the pot off the heat for 20 minutes, then skim the fat and reduce the braising liquid for 15 minutes.

Starter: leek and potato soup
Two hours into the braise, sweat the sliced leeks in butter for 10 minutes. Add the potatoes and the stock and simmer for 30 minutes, then blend the soup smooth; 5 minutes. Hold it warm on the back burner until the guests are seated.

Dessert: apple tart
Roll the pastry and line the tin; 15 minutes. Chill the shell in the refrigerator for at least 30 minutes and no more than two hours. Slice the apples and arrange them in the shell; 20 minutes. Bake the tart for 45 minutes the moment the ribs leave the oven, while the ribs are resting.

Service
Ladle the soup when everyone is seated; ten minutes at the table. Plate the ribs ten minutes after the soup bowls are cleared, and give the plating five minutes. Let the main course run as long as it runs. The tart goes out twenty minutes after the main plates come back; ten minutes to slice and serve.
