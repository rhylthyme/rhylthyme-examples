Holiday Cookie Batch: Three Doughs, One Oven

Three doughs, one stand mixer, one oven and two sheet pans. A pan has to cool before it is reloaded or the next batch spreads, so the oven waits on the pans as often as the pans wait on the oven. One baker, one afternoon, everything boxed by evening.

Doughs
Cream the butter and sugar for the sugar cookies and beat in the flour; 12 minutes in the mixer. Wrap the dough and chill it for 1 hour.
Wash the bowl, then make the chocolate chip dough the same way; 12 minutes. That one goes straight onto a pan.
Wash the bowl again and make the gingerbread dough with molasses and spice; 15 minutes. Chill it for 2 hours.

Order
The oven runs one pan at a time, in this order: chocolate chip, sugar, gingerbread. Nothing goes in until its dough is cut and a cool pan is free.

Baking
Heat the oven to 350°F; 15 minutes. Scoop the chocolate chip dough onto the first pan; 6 minutes. Bake it for 11 minutes. Slide the pan onto a rack and let it cool for 12 minutes before you touch the cookies, and let the pan itself cool for 20 minutes before it goes back in.

Roll and cut the chilled sugar cookies onto the second pan; 14 minutes. Bake for 9 minutes as soon as the chocolate chip pan is out. Let the sugar cookies cool on the rack for 10 minutes; the second pan needs its own 20 minutes before anything else touches it.

Roll the gingerbread a quarter inch thick and cut it out; 18 minutes. Bake for 10 minutes once the sugar cookies are out and the first pan has cooled. Cool the gingerbread for 15 minutes. Ice the gingerbread when it is cold; 25 minutes.

Box everything; 10 minutes.
